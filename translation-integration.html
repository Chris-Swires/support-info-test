Why it matters · 1.5 min

Making translation part of the platform

Today

Translation and authoring are separate. Getting translated content into an extension means moving files by hand, which holds back adoption.

The goal

Customers translate International content in one tool and push it straight into their own extension.

The benefit

Faster, easier national translations. That makes the Managed Service more valuable to members and supports wider use of SNOMED CT.

Phase 1 · January – May 2026 · 2.5 min

Testing showed we needed a better tool

What we did

We connected our existing translation tool, which was based on the open-source product Weblate, to the Authoring Platform.

What we found

In March 2026, testing showed that Weblate could not handle the number of extensions and languages our customers need.

20 min → ~24 h
time taken to add one new language

Found early

We flagged scaling as a risk at the start, and testing confirmed it before any customer relied on the tool.

Nothing wasted

Most of the integration work we built is being reused in Phase 2.

Decision · April 2026 · 2 min

Our response: we built our own Translation Studio

In April 2026 we decided to replace Weblate with Translation Studio, a translation tool built in-house as part of Simplex.

Built to scale

Handles all the extensions and languages customers need.

Same technology

Uses the same technology as our terminology server, Snowstorm.

Lower maintenance

No third-party product to customise and keep up to date.

Our roadmap

We decide the features and workflow, designed for SNOMED CT.

Phase 2 · Architecture · 2.5 min

How the integration fits together

Authoring Platform

Translation project & task in the customer's extension. Link to Translation Studio in the UI.

promote / rebase→MAINT-3172

Snowstorm

Commit hook posts a message to a queue. Fast language-only RF2 export (ISTO-153).

queue message→MAINT-3176

Simplex Translation Studio

Pulls new content, updates translation sets (SIMPLEX-208), translators do the work.

←Approved translations are pushed back into an authoring task for review and promotion (MAINT-2994, MAINT-3003)

Reused from Phase 1

Translation project config, custom field, task creation, export uniqueness check, MS-mode deployment config (MAINT-2999) and MS display (MAINT-3000).

New in Phase 2

Event-driven sync on rebase and promotion, notification when a task is deleted (MAINT-3192), configurable working branch per environment (MAINT-3193).

Guard rails

One task at a time per translation project. Link shown on dev and UAT only until release (MAINT-2990).
