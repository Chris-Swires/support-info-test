On median (typical-request) latency, Netherlands still shows a severe regression, and it surfaces hidden regressions in more extensions than p95 does.
Netherlands is still the standout: median latency up +171% (659 ms → 1,786 ms). But median tells a wider “hidden in the average” story than p95 does: Australia, Denmark, International edition (service as a whole) look fine on blended median but hide a worse individual check. Denmark's blended median looks the healthiest of all (+7%), yet its worst single check by median is the most extreme number on this page — a sign of how noisy median gets on a check with only a handful of samples, not necessarily a real regression. Treat the median view as a second opinion, not a replacement, for exactly that reason.

Methodology & caveats

What to keep in mind before this feeds a hosting decision

▸
  • Single run per configuration. None of these nine runs were repeated, so there's no error bar on any number above — a single slow request lands differently in a p95 of ~100 samples than one of ~1000. Treat the “worst check” percentages as directional until Week 2+ repeats are available.
  • KPI thresholds are placeholders. UKTEST-4 (signed-off thresholds) was still not signed off when this suite was built; the p95<500ms / p99<1000ms thresholds embedded in every run are defaults, not agreed targets. Every run in this dataset fails them, including the UAT baseline — use the before/after percentages above, not the pass/fail flags.
  • Low-sample checks are flagged, not banded. Branch setup/teardown checks run 1–3 times per test and are marked low-n rather than given a colour verdict.
  • “Before” and “after” are also different environments. Before runs hit uat-snowstorm.ihtsdotools.org; after runs hit the new dev-uk-snowstorm.ihtsdotools.org instance. That's the intended test (this is the candidate hosting environment), but any infrastructure difference between the two — not only the UK extension upload — could contribute to the deltas shown here.
  • Verdict bands: WITHIN NOISE p95 change ≤10%   MODERATE REGRESSION 10–50%   SEVERE REGRESSION >50%   a check is left unbanded below 10 samples in before or after.
